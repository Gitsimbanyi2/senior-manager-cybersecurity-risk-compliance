# Information Security Policy Framework

## Policy Hierarchy

```
Board / Executive
    └── Information Security Policy (master)
            ├── Access Control Policy
            ├── Acceptable Use Policy
            ├── Data Classification & Handling Policy
            ├── Incident Response Policy
            ├── Change Management Policy
            ├── Backup & Recovery Policy
            ├── Third-Party Security Policy
            └── Security Awareness & Training Policy
```

## Master Policy — Key Statements

1. **Accountability** — Senior management is accountable for information security
2. **Risk-based approach** — Controls proportional to identified risk
3. **Compliance** — Adherence to applicable laws, regulations, and contracts
4. **Continuous improvement** — Regular review and update of the security program
5. **Roles & responsibilities** — Defined for all personnel and third parties

## Roles & Responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
|------|-----------------|
| Board / Executive | Approve strategy, allocate budget, receive risk reports |
| CISO / Security Manager | Lead program, manage risk, report to executive |
| IT Operations | Implement and maintain technical controls |
| All Employees | Follow policies, complete training, report incidents |
| Internal Audit | Independent assessment of control effectiveness |

## Policy Review Cycle

- Annual review of all policies
- Ad-hoc update triggered by regulatory change, audit finding, or major incident
- Version control with approval signatures and effective dates

## Metrics for Management Reporting

- Number of open critical/high risks
- Mean time to detect (MTTD) and respond (MTTR)
- Compliance audit findings (open vs. closed)
- Security awareness training completion rate
- Vulnerability remediation SLA adherence
